# Test catalogue

Generated by `npm run catalog` from the test names - do not edit by hand.

## Unit (Vitest)

Pure logic, no database.

### `tests/unit/i18n.test.ts`

**Languages**

- open in Indonesian unless the browser prefers English
- have the same texts in English and Indonesian, with the same placeholders
- fill placeholders and write rupiah the local way

### `tests/unit/login-limiter.test.ts`

**Sign-in throttling**

- locks a username on one network after 5 failures, for 5 minutes
- locks a network that tries many usernames (20 failures)
- forgets failures after a successful sign-in, and failures older than 15 minutes
- treats usernames without regard to case

### `tests/unit/money.test.ts`

**Typed amounts**

- read rupiah the way they are written in jastip chats
- refuse what is not an amount
- read percentages with a comma or a dot, up to 100%

### `tests/unit/pricing.test.ts`

**Pricing an order**

- flat fee per item: 'Tas Rp 350.000 + fee 25rb', twice
- percentage fee: '10% dari harga', rounded to whole rupiah per line
- NETT: the price already includes the fee
- bundle: one flat fee for several items, 'bundling 3 item fee 50rb'
- an empty bundle charges nothing
- mixes fee types in one order, then discount, voucher and ongkir
- discount and voucher never take the total below zero, nor reduce ongkir
- ongkir not known yet is left out of the total
- refuses impossible input instead of guessing

### `tests/unit/whatsapp.test.ts`

**WhatsApp numbers**

- accept the ways people write Indonesian numbers
- refuse what is not a phone number
- make a chat link with a ready message, and read nicely

## Integration (Vitest + PostgreSQL)

Against a real PostgreSQL with the real migrations (Testcontainers, or TEST_DATABASE_URL in CI).

### `tests/integration/accounts.test.ts`

**Accounts**

- creates the owner account once from the configured username and password
- signs in with the right password, any case of username, and refuses everything else the same way
- refuses an owner password shorter than 12 characters

**Sessions**

- a session token signs the user in until it expires; only its hash is stored
- signing out ends the session; unknown tokens are refused
- a session in its second half is extended, so a shop in daily use stays signed in

### `tests/integration/bootstrap.test.ts`

**First start**

- creates the owner from OWNER_USERNAME / OWNER_PASSWORD, and nothing else
- in demo mode resets to the sample shop: fake payment accounts, invented clients and orders; everyone is signed out

### `tests/integration/clients.test.ts`

**Clients**

- are saved with their WhatsApp number in international form
- refuse a missing name, a number that is not a phone, and a number another client already has
- can be marked as reseller and edited; several clients may have no number
- are found by part of the name or of the number, newest first, with their order count
- without orders are deleted; with orders they are anonymised and the orders stay

### `tests/integration/orders.test.ts`

**Orders**

- are priced on the server and stored with their lines, bundles and totals
- are numbered per year, TLY-2026-0001 onwards, without gaps or repeats
- take the voucher amount from the shop settings when ticked
- refuse bad input field by field, and an unknown or deleted client
- can be edited: lines are replaced and the totals priced again, the number stays
- are listed newest first and found by number or client name

### `tests/integration/settings.test.ts`

**Shop settings**

- start from the shop's public profile, with no payment accounts until the owner adds them
- are saved and read back, with the Instagram handle and account numbers tidied
- refuse invalid values field by field and keep what was saved
- accept a WhatsApp number, wa.me link or group invite, and at most 5 payment accounts

### `tests/e2e/orders.spec.ts`
