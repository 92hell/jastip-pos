# Test catalogue

Generated by `npm run catalog` from the test names - do not edit by hand.
Totals: 7 unit, 12 integration, 7 end-to-end.

## Unit (Vitest)

Pure logic, no database.

### `tests/unit/i18n.test.ts`

**Languages**

- open in Indonesian unless the browser prefers English
- have the same texts in English and Indonesian, with the same placeholders
- fill placeholders and write rupiah the local way

### `tests/unit/login-limiter.test.ts`

**Sign-in throttling**

- locks a username on one network after 5 failures, for 5 minutes
- locks a network that tries many usernames (20 failures)
- forgets failures after a successful sign-in, and failures older than 15 minutes
- treats usernames without regard to case

## Integration (Vitest + PostgreSQL)

Against a real PostgreSQL with the real migrations (Testcontainers, or TEST_DATABASE_URL in CI).

### `tests/integration/accounts.test.ts`

**Accounts**

- creates the owner account once from the configured username and password
- signs in with the right password, any case of username, and refuses everything else the same way
- refuses an owner password shorter than 12 characters

**Sessions**

- a session token signs the user in until it expires; only its hash is stored
- signing out ends the session; unknown tokens are refused
- a session in its second half is extended, so a shop in daily use stays signed in

### `tests/integration/bootstrap.test.ts`

**First start**

- creates the owner from OWNER_USERNAME / OWNER_PASSWORD, and nothing else
- in demo mode resets to the sample shop with fake payment accounts, and signs everyone out

### `tests/integration/settings.test.ts`

**Shop settings**

- start from the shop's public profile, with no payment accounts until the owner adds them
- are saved and read back, with the Instagram handle and account numbers tidied
- refuse invalid values field by field and keep what was saved
- accept a WhatsApp number, wa.me link or group invite, and at most 5 payment accounts

## End-to-end (Playwright)

### `tests/e2e/settings.spec.ts`

- a refused save keeps what she typed and marks the field; fixing it saves, and the shop page shows it
- payment accounts can be added up to five and removed
