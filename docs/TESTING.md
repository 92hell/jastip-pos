# Test catalogue

Generated by `npm run catalog` from the test names - do not edit by hand.
Totals: 37 unit, 45 integration, 21 end-to-end.

## Unit (Vitest)

Pure logic, no database.

### `tests/unit/balance.test.ts`

**What a client has paid and still owes**

- nothing paid: everything is due, and the DP target is shown
- the DP paid: the rest is due when the items arrive
- less than the DP is a partial payment; with no DP target any payment is partial
- paid in full, in any number of payments
- an item called off after paying leaves the client overpaid, until refunded or moved to credit
- a free order (everything sold out and called off) with nothing paid is settled
- DP amounts are rounded up to whole rupiah

### `tests/unit/i18n.test.ts`

**Languages**

- open in Indonesian unless the browser prefers English
- have the same texts in English and Indonesian, with the same placeholders
- fill placeholders and write rupiah the local way

### `tests/unit/login-limiter.test.ts`

**Sign-in throttling**

- locks a username on one network after 5 failures, for 5 minutes
- locks a network that tries many usernames (20 failures)
- forgets failures after a successful sign-in, and failures older than 15 minutes
- treats usernames without regard to case

### `tests/unit/money.test.ts`

**Typed amounts**

- read rupiah the way they are written in jastip chats
- refuse what is not an amount
- read percentages with a comma or a dot, up to 100%

### `tests/unit/offline-queue.test.ts`

**Ticks made without signal**

- are kept on the phone, one per item: the latest tick wins
- survive closing the app (they are stored, not only in memory)
- are sent in order when back online; what was sent is removed, what failed stays for next time
- drop a change the server refuses for good (e.g. the item was deleted), instead of retrying forever
- ignore damaged stored data, and are cleared on sign-out
- keep a tick made while the earlier ones are being sent

### `tests/unit/pricing.test.ts`

**Pricing an order**

- flat fee per item: 'Tas Rp 350.000 + fee 25rb', twice
- percentage fee: '10% dari harga', rounded to whole rupiah per line
- NETT: the price already includes the fee
- bundle: one flat fee for several items, 'bundling 3 item fee 50rb'
- an empty bundle charges nothing
- mixes fee types in one order, then discount, voucher and ongkir
- discount and voucher never take the total below zero, nor reduce ongkir
- ongkir not known yet is left out of the total
- refuses impossible input instead of guessing
- a sold-out item is not charged: neither its price nor its fee
- a bundle keeps its fee while any of its items is still coming, and drops it when all are sold out

### `tests/unit/whatsapp.test.ts`

**WhatsApp numbers**

- accept the ways people write Indonesian numbers
- refuse what is not a phone number
- make a chat link with a ready message, and read nicely

## Integration (Vitest + PostgreSQL)

Against a real PostgreSQL with the real migrations (Testcontainers, or TEST_DATABASE_URL in CI).

### `tests/integration/accounts.test.ts`

**Accounts**

- creates the owner account once from the configured username and password
- signs in with the right password, any case of username, and refuses everything else the same way
- refuses an owner password shorter than 12 characters

**Sessions**

- a session token signs the user in until it expires; only its hash is stored
- signing out ends the session; unknown tokens are refused
- a session in its second half is extended, so a shop in daily use stays signed in

### `tests/integration/bootstrap.test.ts`

**First start**

- creates the owner from OWNER_USERNAME / OWNER_PASSWORD, and nothing else
- in demo mode resets to the sample shop: fake payment accounts, invented clients and orders; everyone is signed out

### `tests/integration/clients.test.ts`

**Clients**

- are saved with their WhatsApp number in international form
- refuse a missing name, a number that is not a phone, and a number another client already has
- can be marked as reseller and edited; several clients may have no number
- are found by part of the name or of the number, newest first, with their order count
- without orders are deleted; with orders they are anonymised and the orders stay

### `tests/integration/events.test.ts`

**Events (trips, open POs, bazaars, wholesale)**

- are checked: the end is not before the start, and the order deadline is not after the end
- are listed open first (soonest first), then closed, with their orders and how many items are still to buy
- closed events take no new orders; their orders can still be edited, and reopening takes orders again
- without orders can be deleted; with orders they stay
- have a shopping list by store (A–Z, no store last) with what to buy, what is bought and what sold out

### `tests/integration/item-status.test.ts`

**Item status**

- moves requested → bought → arrived → packed, and any step can be undone
- is kept when the order is edited; removed lines go, new lines start as requested
- cannot be changed through another order
- shipping the order ships every item still coming, with courier and tracking number

**Sold-out items**

- are not charged while she asks the client, and stay uncharged if called off
- can be swapped for a similar item: same quantity and fee type, its own price, linked, and priced again
- only sold-out items can be swapped or called off, and the replacement needs a name and a price
- a bundle loses its fee only when all of its items sold out; undoing a sold-out charges again

### `tests/integration/orders.test.ts`

**Orders**

- are priced on the server and stored with their lines, bundles and totals
- are numbered per year, TLY-2026-0001 onwards, without gaps or repeats
- take the voucher amount from the shop settings when ticked
- refuse bad input field by field, and an unknown or deleted client
- can be edited: lines are replaced and the totals priced again, the number stays
- are listed newest first and found by number or client name

### `tests/integration/payments.test.ts`

**Payments**

- a DP then the rest: the balance follows
- keep a photo of the transfer receipt only when it really is an image of at most 800 KB
- refuse an amount that is not a positive rupiah amount, and an impossible date
- a payment entered by mistake can be removed

**Called-off items already paid for**

- leave the client overpaid; she sends the difference back
- or keeps it as credit, which pays part of the client's next order (never more than they have or owe)

**The client's invoice link**

- shows the order, its status and balance, and her payment accounts, but nothing private
- is a 128-bit token; unknown or malformed tokens show nothing; a new link replaces the old one
- every order gets its own link

### `tests/integration/settings.test.ts`

**Shop settings**

- start from the shop's public profile, with no payment accounts until the owner adds them
- are saved and read back, with the Instagram handle and account numbers tidied
- refuse invalid values field by field and keep what was saved
- accept a WhatsApp number, wa.me link or group invite, and at most 5 payment accounts

## End-to-end (Playwright)

Whole journeys in Chromium against the built app in demo mode, at desktop and phone size, with axe accessibility checks.

### `tests/e2e/clients.spec.ts`

- a client is added, found by name or number, and a number another client has is refused (what she typed stays)
- deleting a client who has orders wipes their details but keeps the orders

### `tests/e2e/events.spec.ts`

- an event is checked, takes orders, and closes to new orders until reopened
- the shopping list groups items by store, ticks what is bought and marks what is sold out

### `tests/e2e/orders.spec.ts`

- an order mixing fee types shows its total while typing, saves the same total, and can be edited
- the order form refuses what is not an amount, marks it, and keeps everything typed
- a sold-out item is swapped for a similar one, another is called off, and the order is shipped

### `tests/e2e/payments.spec.ts`

- a DP with a receipt photo, then the rest; the client's invoice link shows the balance but nothing private
- after a paid item is called off, the difference is kept as credit and pays part of the next order
- an unknown invoice link says so, in the client's language

### `tests/e2e/phone.spec.ts`

- every page fits a phone screen
- on a phone the main sections are tabs at the bottom; the rest is under More

### `tests/e2e/pwa.spec.ts`

- the app can be installed: manifest, icons, iPhone home-screen icon, and a service worker that is never cached
- a shopping list opened once still opens and can be ticked without signal; ticks are sent when signal returns
- signing out forgets the saved lists and unsent ticks on this phone

### `tests/e2e/settings.spec.ts`

- a refused save keeps what she typed and marks the field; fixing it saves, and the shop page shows it
- payment accounts can be added up to five and removed

### `tests/e2e/shop-and-sign-in.spec.ts`

- the shop page shows who she is, her rules and the voucher, in Indonesian or English
- a page without a language opens in the browser's language
- a wrong password is refused and keeps the username; the demo admin signs in and out
- admin pages need a sign-in
